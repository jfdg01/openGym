# No-weak-links strength program

A long-term strength program, logged in openGym, in which every listed movement gets measurably stronger.

## Language

**Movement**:
One joint action against a resistance, such as hip internal rotation with the hip extended. The program counts movements, not muscles.
_Avoid_: muscle, lift, pattern

**Exercise**:
A specific implement and set-up that trains exactly one primary movement, such as cable hip internal rotation. openGym logs exercises. A compound exercise gives no credit to its secondary movements.
_Avoid_: movement (for the logged thing), drill

**Exposure**:
One session in which a movement gets at least one hard set.
_Avoid_: workout, session (when the movement is meant)

**Weak link**:
A strength movement whose trend is worse than the others by more than the noise of its measurement. A single low test is not a weak link.
_Avoid_: lagging muscle, imbalance

**Skill item**:
A movement that is logged but kept out of the weak-link ranking, such as toe abduction.
_Avoid_: mobility drill
